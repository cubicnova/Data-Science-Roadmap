# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The CubicNova Data Science Roadmap community is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive learning environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal circumstances.

We want this repository to be a place where people can learn, ask questions, share ideas, improve educational materials, and collaborate on practical Data Science and AI projects.

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in this project a positive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Welcoming beginners and answering questions constructively.
- Giving clear, actionable, and respectful feedback.
- Discussing technical disagreements using evidence and reasoning.
- Accepting constructive criticism and suggestions for improvement.
- Recognizing other people's contributions and giving appropriate credit.
- Respecting different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Keeping discussions focused on learning, collaboration, and the project.
- Helping maintain accurate, accessible, and inclusive educational resources.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning language or conduct.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated hostile arguments.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexually explicit harassment.
- Spam, scams, or repeated unrelated promotional content.
- Deliberately sabotaging contributions or discussions.
- Plagiarism, misrepresenting others' work, or knowingly removing required attribution.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation hostile or unsafe.

These standards apply to project spaces and public interactions when someone is representing or materially participating in the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and discussions.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Commit messages and repository documentation.
- Contributions to lessons, exercises, projects, notebooks, and diagrams.
- Community spaces used for project-related communication.

It also applies when a person is acting as a project representative in public spaces. Examples include using an official project account or representing the project at an event.

## Respectful Technical Collaboration

Technical debate is welcome. Contributors may disagree about algorithms, tools, explanations, architecture, or teaching methods.

When disagreements occur:

1. Focus on the technical issue rather than the individual.
2. Explain the reasoning and provide evidence or references when useful.
3. Ask questions in good faith.
4. Accept that more than one approach may be reasonable.
5. Keep the discussion constructive and relevant to the project.

Questions from beginners are welcome. Nobody is expected to know every concept before contributing.

## Educational Content, Attribution, and Safety

Because this repository shares learning materials, contributors are expected to:

- Aim for technical accuracy and clearly state assumptions or limitations.
- Credit original authors and sources when required.
- Respect copyright, licenses, and dataset terms.
- Avoid sharing credentials, private information, or confidential data.
- Avoid presenting unsupported claims as established facts.
- Keep examples appropriate for an educational repository.
- Explain potentially risky technical demonstrations responsibly and within lawful, authorized contexts.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards fairly and consistently. They may remove, edit, reject, or request changes to contributions or comments that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Any action should be proportionate to the behavior and circumstances.

Maintainers are expected to communicate moderation decisions respectfully and, where appropriate, explain the reason for an action.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it through an appropriate project communication channel, such as a private message to a repository maintainer if one is available.

When reporting an incident, include as much relevant information as you safely can:

- A description of what happened.
- Links to relevant issues, comments, pull requests, or other project spaces.
- Approximate dates or context.
- Any additional information that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Please avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly. Share only information needed to address the concern.

Reports should be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Maintainers should not disclose report details beyond those who need them to respond, except where disclosure is required for safety or legal reasons.

If the issue involves an immediate threat or danger, contact the appropriate local emergency or support service.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may respond to violations using one or more of the following measures, depending on severity and context:

1. **Correction request:** Ask the person to edit or clarify a comment or contribution.
2. **Warning:** Explain the behavior that must stop.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Temporarily limit participation in relevant project spaces.
4. **Removal:** Remove offending content or reject a contribution.
5. **Longer-term or permanent restriction:** Restrict participation for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers will consider the circumstances, impact, history of behavior, and safety of affected community members. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is not acceptable.

## No Retaliation

Anyone who raises a concern in good faith or helps resolve a conduct issue should be able to do so without intimidation or retaliation. Retaliation may itself be treated as a violation of this Code of Conduct.
