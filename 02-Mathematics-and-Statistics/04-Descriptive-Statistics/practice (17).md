# Descriptive Statistics — Practice Exercises

Show calculations for numerical questions. For Python tasks, use pandas or NumPy where requested and note whether statistics are sample- or population-based.

1. Calculate the mean of [4, 6, 8, 10].
2. Calculate the median of [3, 7, 9, 11, 15].
3. Calculate the median of [2, 4, 8, 10].
4. Find the mode of [1, 2, 2, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5].
5. Calculate the range of [12, 5, 9, 21, 7].
6. Explain how an extreme value can affect the mean and median differently.
7. Calculate the population variance and population standard deviation of [2, 4, 6].
8. Calculate the sample variance of [2, 4, 6].
9. Explain the difference between population variance and sample variance.
10. Find Q1, median, Q3, and IQR for a simple sorted dataset of your choice and state your quartile convention.
11. Explain the 1.5×IQR rule for potential outliers.
12. Given Q1=10 and Q3=18, calculate the IQR and the lower and upper 1.5×IQR fences.
13. A category occurs 18 times in 60 observations. Calculate its relative frequency and percentage.
14. Create a frequency table for ['red', 'blue', 'red', 'green', 'blue', 'red'].
15. Explain the difference between a histogram and a bar chart.
16. Describe what right-skewed data means and how mean and median often compare.
17. Explain what a Pearson correlation of -0.85 indicates and what it does not prove.
18. Calculate the mean and median of [2, 3, 3, 4, 18] and compare them.
19. Use pandas to display descriptive statistics for a numeric Series.
20. Use pandas to calculate the sample variance and standard deviation of a list.
21. Use NumPy to calculate population variance and standard deviation.
22. Create a DataFrame with two numeric columns and calculate their means.
23. Find missing-value counts in each column of a pandas DataFrame.
24. Calculate category proportions from a pandas Series of labels.
25. Use pandas to calculate the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles.
26. Detect potential outliers using the IQR fences in Python.
27. Explain why an outlier should not be deleted automatically.
28. Create a small dataset where the mean and median are equal.
29. Create a small dataset with a right-skewed distribution.
30. Explain why sample size should accompany a summary statistic.
31. Calculate the relative frequency of each category in a list and verify proportions sum to 1.
32. Use pandas `describe()` and explain what count, mean, std, min, and max represent.
33. Explain why a correlation near zero does not rule out a nonlinear relationship.
34. Write a function that returns mean, median, range, and sample standard deviation for a numeric list.
35. Compare mean and standard deviation with median and IQR for a dataset containing an extreme value.

## Challenge

Create a DataFrame with a numeric column containing at least one extreme value. Compare mean, median, standard deviation, IQR, and a boxplot or histogram. Explain which summaries best describe the typical observation and spread.
