# Practice Questions: Sampling

Try the exercises before consulting `solutions.md`. Use Python and NumPy for the programming tasks.

1. Define population, sample, sampling frame, parameter, and statistic.
2. Explain the difference between a population parameter and a sample statistic.
3. What is a sampling unit? Give an example.
4. Describe simple random sampling and one situation where it is useful.
5. How does systematic sampling work? Why should the frame order be checked?
6. Explain stratified sampling and give one reason to use it.
7. Explain cluster sampling and identify one cost advantage and one statistical limitation.
8. What is multistage sampling? Give an example with two stages.
9. Distinguish probability sampling from non-probability sampling.
10. Define convenience sampling and give one limitation.
11. What is purposive sampling, and when might it be appropriate?
12. Explain quota sampling and how it differs from stratified random sampling.
13. What is snowball sampling, and what kind of population might require it?
14. Explain sampling variability in your own words.
15. Explain sampling bias and why increasing sample size may not eliminate it.
16. Give an example of coverage error in an online survey.
17. Name three sources of survey error other than random sampling variability.
18. Why should a sampling frame be current and relevant to the target population?
19. Write the formula for the sample mean and calculate it for [2, 4, 6, 8].
20. Write the formula for sample variance and explain why it uses n - 1 in the denominator.
21. A sample has standard deviation 12 and size 36. Calculate the estimated standard error of its mean.
22. Approximately how does standard error change if sample size is multiplied by four, all else equal?
23. Interpret a 95% confidence interval correctly in terms of repeated sampling.
24. When is a t-critical value generally preferred to 1.96 for a mean confidence interval?
25. What factors should be considered when planning a sample size?
26. Calculate approximate sample size for a mean with z*=1.96, sigma=10, and margin of error E=2. Round up.
27. Use NumPy to select 8 unique values from the integers 1 through 50.
28. Use NumPy to select a random sample of 12 records from a list without replacement.
29. Create a population with 80 members of group A and 20 of group B. Draw a stratified sample of 10 from each group.
30. Simulate 500 samples of size 30 from a population and plot the distribution of their sample means.
31. Compare the sample mean and standard error for sample sizes 10, 50, and 200 from the same population.
32. Write a Python function returning sample size, mean, sample standard deviation, and standard error.
33. Explain why a convenience sample of social media followers may not represent all customers.
34. Propose a sampling strategy for estimating student satisfaction across a university with multiple departments. Justify it.
35. Choose a real-world study, define its population and sampling frame, select a method, and describe two potential biases.
