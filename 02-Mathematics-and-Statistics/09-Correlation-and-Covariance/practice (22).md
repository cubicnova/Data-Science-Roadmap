# Practice Questions: Correlation and Covariance

Try these questions before consulting `solutions.md`. Use NumPy, pandas, SciPy, and Matplotlib for coding exercises.

1. Define covariance and explain what its sign tells you.
2. Write the formula for sample covariance and explain why the denominator is n - 1.
3. How does changing measurement units affect covariance?
4. Define Pearson's correlation coefficient and state its possible range.
5. What does a correlation of +1 indicate?
6. What does a correlation of -1 indicate?
7. What does a Pearson correlation near zero mean, and what does it not guarantee?
8. Why is correlation unitless while covariance has units?
9. Explain why correlation does not imply causation.
10. Give an example of a confounding variable that could influence two correlated variables.
11. Distinguish Pearson correlation from Spearman correlation.
12. When might Kendall's tau be useful?
13. Why should a scatter plot be examined before choosing a correlation measure?
14. How can an outlier affect Pearson correlation?
15. Describe a nonlinear relationship that could have Pearson correlation close to zero.
16. What is a covariance matrix?
17. What is a correlation matrix, and what appears on its diagonal for non-constant variables?
18. What happens when one variable is constant and its correlation with another variable is calculated?
19. Interpret a positive covariance between study hours and exam score.
20. Interpret a negative correlation between outdoor temperature and heating demand.
21. Calculate Pearson correlation using NumPy for arrays [1, 2, 3, 4] and [2, 4, 6, 8].
22. Use NumPy to calculate the sample covariance of arrays [1, 2, 3] and [2, 4, 5].
23. Use SciPy to calculate Pearson, Spearman, and Kendall correlation for two numeric arrays.
24. Create a scatter plot for two positively related variables and label both axes.
25. Create a scatter plot for a negative linear relationship and calculate its Pearson correlation.
26. Generate a U-shaped relationship and compare its scatter plot with its Pearson correlation.
27. Create a pandas DataFrame with three numeric columns and calculate its correlation matrix.
28. Use Python to compare Pearson and Spearman correlations for data with an outlier.
29. Explain why a high correlation between two predictor variables may matter in regression.
30. Explain the difference between correlation strength and statistical significance.
31. Given r = 0.8, describe direction and strength without claiming causation.
32. Given r = -0.15, explain a cautious interpretation.
33. List three data-quality checks to perform before computing correlations.
34. Explain why a correlation found among many tested feature pairs may occur by chance.
35. Choose two real-world variables, hypothesize their relationship, choose a correlation method, and describe two limitations.
